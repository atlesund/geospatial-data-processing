## Performance Context

With spatial indexing:
- Index build time: ~2 seconds for 50k features (O(m log m), one-time)

- Total: ~10 seconds (vs. hours/days with naive iteration)

Without spatial indexing (current):
- Edge penalty calculation: ~26 billion checks (500k × 30k lakes + 500k × 23k rivers)
- Estimated time: Hours to days (system hangs)
